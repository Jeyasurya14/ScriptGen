# How to Reach More Users – ScriptGen

Practical ways to grow traffic and signups for your YouTube script generator.

---

## 1. SEO (free, long-term)

- **Target keywords** you already rank for or could rank for:
  - "YouTube script generator", "AI script writer", "free script generator"
  - "Tamil script generator", "Thanglish script writer", "Hindi video script"
  - "YouTube SEO tool", "video script with chapters"
- **Add more content** that matches intent:
  - Blog posts: "How to write a YouTube script in 10 minutes", "Best AI tools for YouTubers", "Script structure for viral Shorts"
  - Landing pages: e.g. `/free-script-generator`, `/ai-script-writer` (you have these – keep improving titles and content)
- **Internal linking**: From every blog post and landing page, link to the app and to other relevant posts.
- **Technical**: You already have canonicals, sitemap, meta. Keep URLs clean and ensure core pages are indexed (Search Console).

---

## 2. Content & blog (free, compounds over time)

- Publish **1–2 posts per month** on:
  - Script writing tips, YouTube growth, Shorts hooks, SEO for videos
  - "ScriptGen vs …" or "How we generate scripts" (transparency builds trust)
- **Repurpose**: Turn a post into a short Twitter/LinkedIn thread, or a simple carousel for Instagram.
- **Calls to action**: Every post should have a clear CTA: "Try ScriptGen – 30 free tokens" with link to `/app`.
- **Guest posts** on creator/YouTube/marketing blogs with a byline link to ScriptGen.

---

## 3. Communities (free, high intent)

- **Reddit**: r/YouTubeCreators, r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube, r/IndianYouTube (or regional subs). Answer questions about scripts, SEO, and tools; mention ScriptGen when it genuinely fits. No spam.
- **Discord / Facebook groups**: Join YouTube creator and content-creator groups. Help first, share the tool when relevant.
- **Quora / similar**: Answer "How do I write a YouTube script?" and similar; link to ScriptGen in the answer or profile.

---

## 4. Referral program (you already have it)

- **Surface it more**: After first script, show a small banner: "Invite friends – you both get 15 tokens" with copy link.
- **In-app reminder**: e.g. when tokens are low: "Share your link and get 15 tokens when friends sign up."
- **Email**: If you add email later, send "Share ScriptGen, get 15 tokens each" to active users.
- **Simple reward**: Consider a small bonus (e.g. extra tokens) when a referred user generates their first script, to encourage quality invites.

---

## 5. Social & word of mouth (free / low cost)

- **Twitter/X**: Post tips on script writing, YouTube growth, and AI for creators. Link to ScriptGen and blog posts. Engage with creator and #YouTubeCreators hashtags.
- **LinkedIn**: If targeting B2B or “professional creators”, share short posts on content strategy and script automation; link to app and blog.
- **YouTube**: Short demo (2–3 min): "How I write scripts in 2 minutes with AI" using ScriptGen. Link in description and pinned comment.
- **Testimonials**: Ask happy users for a short quote; use them on the homepage and in ads later.

---

## 6. Paid (when you have budget)

- **Google Ads**: Search campaigns on "YouTube script generator", "AI script writer", "free script generator" (and Tamil/Hindi if relevant). Send traffic to `/app` or a dedicated landing page.
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)**: Target interests like YouTube creator, content creator, video marketing. Creative: "Write a full YouTube script in 30 seconds" with a short video.
- **YouTube ads**: Skippable ads on creator/script-writing videos; CTA to try ScriptGen.
- Start with a small daily budget, measure signups and cost per signup, then scale what works.

---

## 7. Partnerships & integrations

- **Creator / educator collabs**: Offer free tokens or a custom plan in exchange for a video, post, or email mention.
- **Tool directories**: List ScriptGen on Product Hunt, BetaList, G2, Capterra, "AI tools for creators" lists, and Indian startup directories.
- **Newsletters**: Sponsor or get featured in creator/YouTube/marketing newsletters (e.g. "Tool of the week").

---

## 9. What to measure

- **Traffic**: Google Analytics or Vercel Analytics – sessions, sources (organic, direct, referral, social).
- **Signups**: Sign-ins (e.g. Google) per day/week; trend over time.
- **Activation**: Users who generate at least one script (goal: increase this %).
- **Referrals**: Number of referral signups and tokens granted (you have the data in DB).
- **SEO**: Search Console – queries, clicks, impressions; index status of key pages.

Focus on **signups** and **first script generated** as main outcomes; use traffic and referral stats to see which channels bring the right users.
